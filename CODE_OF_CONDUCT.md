# Code of conduct

Loqui is a collaborative project. Everyone participating in its issues, pull requests, reviews, and project spaces is expected to help make those spaces welcoming and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, regardless of background, identity, ability, or experience.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work. Assume people may have different context and ask for clarification respectfully.
- Respect boundaries and privacy. Get consent before sharing someone else's personal information or private correspondence.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope, safety, and moderation, while discussing disagreements in good faith.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, stalking, doxxing, impersonation, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Neither are spam or sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a project-space concern, contact the maintainers through the repository. If no private contact is listed, open a brief issue asking for a private moderation contact without including names, sensitive details, or evidence in public. You may also use GitHub's built-in reporting and blocking tools. Do not post private information in a public report.

Maintainers may remove content, request changes in behavior, restrict participation, or ban participants when needed to protect the community. The response depends on the severity and context; immediate action may be taken for serious threats or abuse. Reports should be handled discreetly, but the project cannot promise absolute confidentiality or a specific response time.

This policy applies to project-managed spaces and to people acting as representatives of Loqui. It is maintained under the repository's MIT license.
