How to use this.

Each card is one shipped pick from the UI/UX overhaul spec (IDs match docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-loqui-uiux-overhaul.md). Play the demo, then hit Tweak this on anything you want to change and write what's off in the notes box. Flags and notes are saved in this browser. Export tweak list gives you a Markdown list to hand back.

Use ½× / ¼× to slow every token-driven animation down for inspection, Plum Night to check the dark theme, and Reduced motion to see the fallback behaviour. The brand mark is a vector stand-in and mic levels are simulated. Native window resizing isn't modelled.

Trigger animations · the voice pill

Everything that happens from the moment the dictation shortcut fires. The pill lives in a fixed 208×120 native window docked bottom-right with a 12px inset. No footprint is wider than 184px.

Press → record → paste: the full trigger sequence

On main · Plan 04

Hold the button (or focus it and hold Space) to dictate. A press starts expanding at once (300ms, ease-std) and the bars land one expansion later. Words condense above the pill. On release it goes Transcribing (orange comet), then Cleaning up (orange→magenta comet), then Pasted with the drawn check on the bounce spring, and back to idle.

Condensation: words rise above the bars

D3 · VO-R6 B

Cancel is instant: “Discarded · Undo”

On main · Plan 04

No confirmation dialog. A 2px orange underline counts down the hold window: 5s, or 10s for takes over 30s. Hovering the pill pauses it, and Undo restores the take. This countdown runs in real time; slow-motion doesn't apply because it's a functional deadline.
