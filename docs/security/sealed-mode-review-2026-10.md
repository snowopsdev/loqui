What holds: the JavaScript layers of 07a hold up under adversarial probing.

- Layer A, Layer B's DNS, UDP, TLS, fetch and Chromium judge, the spawn guard, privacy IPC authority, the fail-closed state, the recorder sanitizer and renderer honesty all hold.
- No hostile input made main or the renderer show a verified seal or `nothingLeaves`.

What fails is narrower, and there are two kinds:

- **Gaps in the Node backstop's coverage of unusual call forms** (T18-01, T18-03, T18-04, K6).
- **Contract violations around outside-boundary handoffs and copy** (K1, K3, K5).

Everything Chromium-runtime, packaged or native is NOT VERIFIED.

### Strengths (brief)

- **Fail-closed defaults hold everywhere I probed:**
  - `isSealed()` throwing or unconfigured is sealed;
  - a corrupt, empty, BOM-prefixed, directory or unreadable `privacy.json` boots sealed;
  - boot installs every guard before any helper module loads.
- **The attestor cannot be talked into success.** `nothingLeaves: false`, `effectiveSince: null` and `phase` never `"sealed"` hold even when its inputs carry hostile `nothingLeaves: true` / `effectiveSince` values.
- **Privacy IPC re-checks the sender after the native dialog,** and a seal request supersedes a pending unseal.
- **Seal transitions abort tickets synchronously,** sever Node sockets, and refuse every new transport afterwards.
